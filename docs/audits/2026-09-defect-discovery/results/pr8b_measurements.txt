2. Is the gain drift LightGBM's text format?
   instrument: pr8b_gain_precision.py  output: pr8b_gain_precision.txt

   The fold-0 booster against Booster(model_str=booster.model_to_string()):
   the text writes split_gain= with at most 6 significant digits
   ("4.87775 3.24358 ..."). The pickled adapter's gain equals the text round
   trip's exactly in all three tasks; predictions and OOF are bit-identical
   after load. Largest relative gain error observed: 1.040e-06. Bound from the
   format: 5e-6 (half a unit in the 6th significant digit, carried through a
   sum of non-negative split gains).
